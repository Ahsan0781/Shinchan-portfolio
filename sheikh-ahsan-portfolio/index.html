About Me

Design & documents, handled by one dependable freelancer.

I'm Sheikh Ahsan, a freelance web designer, landing page developer, graphic designer, and document formatting specialist. For the past several years I've partnered with founders, agencies, and professionals who need their digital presence — and their paperwork — to look as sharp as the work they do.

What makes my process different is range: I don't just hand you a homepage and disappear. I design the website, build the landing page that converts, create the brand assets around it, and format the business documents that back it up — resumes, proposals, fillable PDF forms — all under one roof, with one point of contact.

  • Direct communication, no account managers
  • Design + development + documents in one hire
  • Fast, transparent turnaround times
  • Revisions until it's pixel-perfect
